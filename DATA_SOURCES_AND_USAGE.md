# Data sources and usage disclaimer

Historical S&P 500 Price Index reference data in the private research pipeline came from CRSP/WRDS and FRED, with documented source transitions and a separately adjudicated 2019 price conflict. The final Live P0/window used Yahoo Finance `^GSPC` daily **Close**, not an ETF, adjusted close or total-return series. VIX observations came from Cboe; 10-year, 3-month and 2-year Treasury rates came from the U.S. Treasury. The private source capture records include URLs, retrieval times and SHA-256 hashes. These providers retain their respective third-party rights; this disclaimer grants no redistribution permission.

No original provider downloads, continuous historical observation tables, derived historical training matrix or Live raw captures are included here. The saved final result JSON does contain the single forecast's P0 and seven Live factor values; it does not provide a reconstructable historical series. Authorised researchers must obtain data directly from the relevant providers under their own access terms, preserve raw files and retrieval metadata, then verify the local source versions and time boundaries before reconstructing X–Y. Obtaining a current historical download does not prove its first availability at a past forecast cutoff. Source-specific historical availability was a **Proxy PIT research assumption**; Strict PIT is UNKNOWN. Because the excluded original data versions are necessary, this public repository is a saved-result verification and method record rather than an offline end-to-end reproduction package.

Public source entry points: [FRED SP500](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SP500), [Yahoo Finance ^GSPC](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/%5EGSPC/), [Cboe VIX](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/vix_historical_data/), [U.S. Treasury yield rates](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/pages/textview?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve). CRSP/WRDS access requires the authorised institutional channel.
