# Seven-factor coefficient sign and stability method

The corrected comparison's `frozen_training_membership.csv` lists every training origin ID and SHA-256 for each fit, while `predictions.csv` stores predictions but no fold coefficients. We therefore refitted only the seven-factor `StandardScaler → Ridge(alpha=1, solver=auto)` with the **exact saved member IDs**. Before retaining any coefficient, the code checked each training row's own historical feature gate, label maturity against the prediction cutoff, ID count/hash, and numerical reproduction of the saved return (absolute tolerance 1e-10). All 413 predictions reproduced. Standardized coefficients are directly comparable across fits; `raw_unit_slope` is also stored but scales differ by fold.

Signs were counted separately for 257 G3 and 156 G4 origins, with mean, median, range and annual positive shares in `coefficient_sign_stability.csv`; every individual fit is in `coefficient_refit_by_origin.csv`. G3's three Ridge/OLS specifications use a preceding-year-end training-origin cap, so many same-year fits share members and coefficients: 257 signs must not be described as 257 independent confirmations. G4 fits expand weekly. Correlated rates and risk variables make conditional signs unstable in economic interpretation.

The report's working directions were specified **after some coefficients were seen**: RET5 +, RET20 +, RV20 +, VIX +, UST10Y −, UST3M −, SPREAD +. The refits find RET5 negative and UST3M positive at every evaluated origin in both periods, against those working directions. VIX positive, UST10Y negative and SPREAD positive at every origin. RET20 and RV20 change sign across G3 years; both are positive in G4. These are descriptive conditional regression coefficients, not preregistered hypotheses or causal effects.
